\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\subsection{Identity and credentials}
W3C Decentralized Identifiers and Verifiable Credentials provide identifier and claim data models
\cite{w3c-did,w3c-vc}. Non-transferability proposals such as EIP-5192 and EIP-4973 explore
account-bound attestations \cite{eip-5192,eip-4973}, while ERC-4337 supports programmable account
logic \cite{eip-4337}. These primitives do not by themselves establish unique personhood, legal
responsibility, truthful behavior, or fair adjudication. Recent agent-identity proposals emphasize
zero-trust controls and verifiable bindings \cite{zero-trust,binding-id}; AgentTrust uses a much
weaker on-chain address binding in the current prototype.

\subsection{Reputation, attestations, and insured agents}
Ethereum Attestation Service supplies general attestation infrastructure \cite{eas}, and recent work
studies blockchain agent reputation and registered-agent datasets \cite{agent-rep,agent-dataset}.
Insured Agents proposes a market in which specialized insurers post collateral and price agent risk
\cite{insured-agents}. Our prototype is related, but its scalar score is only a heuristic outcome
summary. The inspected contracts do not estimate default probability, quote premiums, or prevent
wash trading and score farming. Any reputation-to-price equation in Section~\ref{sec:pricing} is a
modeling hypothesis, not implemented underwriting.

\subsection{Decentralized dispute resolution}
Kleros, Aragon Court, and UMA use staking, challenge, and/or coordination incentives in deployed or
documented dispute systems \cite{kleros,aragon,uma}. Nexus Mutual illustrates capital pooling and
claims assessment \cite{nexus-mutual}. These systems show that stake can make identities and actions
costly, but they do not make truth a dominant strategy: bribery, voter selection, information quality,
participation, governance, and correlated failures remain central. AgentTrust currently implements a
single-round open vote without random jury selection or an appeal layer.

\subsection{Agent trust infrastructure}
Recent work surveys blockchain-based trustworthy agent networks and agent-to-agent finance
\cite{agent-networks,a2a-finance}. ERC-8004 specifies interoperable agent registries
\cite{erc8004}. These works motivate combining identity, payment, verification, and reputation.
We make no priority or ``first system'' claim. The relevant design space is active, and local prior
work already includes closely related identity, insurance, escrow, and governance components.
AgentTrust should therefore be read as one concrete composition and experimental test bed rather than
as a uniquely complete or production-proven protocol.
